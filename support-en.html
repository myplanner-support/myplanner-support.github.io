MyPlanner Support

Find answers to common questions below. If you need help, send us an email.

How does the free trial work?

After you start the trial, every MyPlanner feature is free for 30 days. There is no subscription or automatic charge.

When the trial ends, you can unlock full access with a single purchase. The current price is shown in the app.

What happens to my records after 30 days?

Your records stay in MyPlanner. You can still view events, lists and habits, and export a backup.

Creating, editing and marking records as complete requires the full version.

How do I restore a purchase?

Tap “Restore Purchases” in MyPlanner. Make sure you use the same Apple Account you used to buy the full version.

If the purchase does not appear, check your internet connection or email us.

Where are my records stored?

Your records stay on your iPhone. You can create a backup in MyPlanner Settings and save it wherever you choose.

MyPlanner does not automatically sync records between devices.

How can I contact support?

Email us at myplanner.app.support@gmail.com.

Briefly describe your question or issue. If you are reporting a problem, include your MyPlanner and iOS versions if possible. Do not send passwords, payment card details or health information.
