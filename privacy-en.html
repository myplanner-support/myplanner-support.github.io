Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Your records

Your events, tasks, lists, habits, completion history, templates and MyPlanner settings are stored on your iPhone. You do not need to create a MyPlanner account.

We do not receive the contents of your records. MyPlanner does not sync them between devices.

Access to iPhone data

Apple Health. With your permission, MyPlanner can use your step count, exercise minutes and completed workouts to mark habits as done. This information is not sent to us or included in backups. MyPlanner does not change data in Apple Health.

Calendars. With your permission, MyPlanner shows events from selected iPhone calendars in the app calendar, on Home and in the events widget. A limited preview (event title, time and calendar) is stored temporarily on your iPhone for the widget and is not included in backups. We do not receive this information or edit events in their original calendars. You may need to open MyPlanner to refresh the widget.

Notifications and Siri. MyPlanner uses iPhone features for reminders and voice commands. Reminder text may appear on your Lock Screen, depending on your device settings.

Managing access. You can turn off MyPlanner’s access to Health, calendars and notifications at any time in your iPhone settings. You can also manage Health permissions in the Health app.

Free trial and purchases

After you start the free trial, all features are available for 30 days. You can then unlock full access with a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions or automatic charges.

The App Store handles purchases and refunds. MyPlanner checks your purchase status to unlock paid features, but does not receive your payment card details.

Backups

You choose when to create a backup and where to save it. If you save or share a backup using another app or service, that service's privacy rules apply. We do not receive your backups.

Deleting data

You can delete records in the app. Deleted tasks and lists may remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.

Uninstalling MyPlanner removes its local records from your iPhone. Backups you saved separately remain wherever you stored them. Apple keeps purchase records under its own policies.

Advertising and analytics

MyPlanner has no ads, advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Apple services, including the App Store and iPhone diagnostics, follow Apple's privacy policies.

Support messages and privacy questions

MyPlanner is developed by Ivan Kukin, who is responsible for the app and responding to enquiries. Contact us at myplanner.app.support@gmail.com.

If you email support, we use your email address and message to respond to your request. Messages are stored by our email service for as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your correspondence by writing to the same address.

We will update this page if the way MyPlanner uses information changes.
